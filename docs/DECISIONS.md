## D014 · 2026-11-04 · pivot (plan §9) · both alternative directions declined; no H002 launched
- Evidence: docs/prior_art/pivot_review.md (bounded agent review); src/physics_ml/amr1d.py + tests/test_pivot_equivalence.py (7 passed, <1 s CPU).
- Direction 1 (online order changes in a passive learned reservoir): certified passive truncation with H∞ bounds (Breiten–Morandin–Schulze 2012.15266), time-varying-order error bounds (Sandberg–Rantzer 2004) and energy tanks for state-dimension jumps (Restrepo et al., Automatica 2025, abstract only) are direct precedents; safe order reduction is only possible in quiet input, where exact unforced propagation is already nearly free — the same trap measured in D011. Declined.
- Direction 2 (jointly represented conservative fluxes across changing hierarchies): exact conservation is classical (Berger–Colella refluxing + conservative regrid). The review's proposed first hypothesis (coarse face flux defined as the fine time/subface sum) was checked against its own rejection criterion (d3) analytically and numerically: with matched ghost treatment it is algebraically identical to refluxing for ANY flux function — max state difference 2.2e-16 (Rusanov) and 6.7e-16 (random MLP flux) after 200 steps with dynamic regridding; mass drift 1.1e-16. The only difference (≈9e-4) is classical ghost-cell time interpolation, which the joint form cannot use. A remaining "accuracy of learned flux in the loop" study would be an architecture benchmark adjacent to CLNO/LAMP+refluxing with high novelty risk, not a test of a distinct mechanism. Declined without spending training compute.
- Decision: research disposition = supported negative result for H001 (pilot scope) plus documented pivot assessment; no third hypothesis is implemented without new evidence or user direction (plan §9: do not run architecture families merely to generate activity). Remaining work: seeds 1–2 (C8 uncertainty), P5 robustness on seed models, adversarial review, final RESULTS.
